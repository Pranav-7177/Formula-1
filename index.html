THE SPORT

What is Formula 1?

The Pinnacle of Motorsport

Formula 1 is the highest class of international single-seater motor racing. It combines advanced engineering, elite driving skill and strategic competition.

Teams compete at circuits around the world, developing highly sophisticated cars while drivers fight for championship points.

Every race combines speed, strategy, technology and teamwork to create one of the most demanding forms of motorsport.

PROFILE

Formula 1 Essentials

01 / CARS

Engineering at Speed

Aerodynamics • Power • Precision

Modern Formula 1 cars are designed around aerodynamics, lightweight construction, hybrid power technology and advanced engineering.

Teams continuously develop their cars throughout a season to gain performance advantages over their competitors.

02 / DRIVERS

The Drivers

Skill • Focus • Reaction

Formula 1 drivers require exceptional concentration, physical fitness, technical understanding and racecraft.

Drivers work closely with their engineers and teams to understand car performance and develop race strategies.

03 / CIRCUITS

Iconic Circuits

Corners • Straights • Challenges

Formula 1 visits circuits with very different characteristics. Some reward top speed, while others demand precision through technical corners.

Each circuit creates a different engineering and strategic challenge for teams and drivers.
